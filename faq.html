Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to list a business?

The launch fee is ₦1,000. It is used to activate the vendor's listing after successful payment confirmation.

Will I definitely get customers?

No. We never promise guaranteed customers. The platform helps customers discover you and can send relevant customer requests.

Who can become an ambassador?

Any eligible person can create an ambassador account and refer genuine business owners.

When does an ambassador earn?

A referral reward is recorded when the referred vendor completes a successful paid registration and the referral is eligible.

How do customers contact businesses?

Active listings can show the business's supplied phone or WhatsApp details. Customers can also post a request for relevant businesses to respond.

Can a business edit its profile?

Yes. Vendors can update their business profile and products from their dashboard.

Is the platform available nationwide?

The architecture is designed for Nigeria. The first rollout can start locally and expand as the business directory grows.
