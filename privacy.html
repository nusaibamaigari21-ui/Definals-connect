Privacy Notice

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Information we collect

We may collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, business information, account credentials in protected form, referral relationships, payment references and customer request information needed to operate the platform.

How we use information

We use information to create accounts, publish business listings, process payments, operate referrals, match customer requests, provide support, prevent abuse and improve the service.

Payments

Payment processing is handled through Paystack. Definals Connect should not store raw card details. Payment references and transaction status may be stored for reconciliation and account activation.

Sharing

Public business profile information is visible to platform visitors. Customer request details may be shared with relevant businesses so they can respond. We do not sell personal information as a business model.

Security

We use reasonable technical controls including password hashing, protected authentication cookies, rate limiting and signed payment webhooks. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Retention and choices

We retain information as reasonably necessary for account operation, payment reconciliation, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Contact platform support to request account-related changes or deletion where applicable.

Third parties

Third-party services such as payment, hosting and database providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Important

This is a platform launch template, not legal advice. Before public launch, have the final notice reviewed for Nigerian data-protection requirements and your actual providers.
